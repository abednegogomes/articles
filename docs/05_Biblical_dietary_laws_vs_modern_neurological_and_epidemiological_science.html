Biblical Dietary Laws vs. Modern Neurological & Epidemiological Science

An analysis mapping the dietary classifications in Deuteronomy 14 and Leviticus 11 onto modern scientific data regarding neocortical animal intelligence and zoonotic disease vector risks.

1. Clean Animals (Permitted to Eat)

Verse (AMP) Animals Intelligence Level & Context Disease Risk Pandemic & Epidemic History
Deut 14:4-6
These are the animals that you may eat: the ox, the sheep, the goat, the deer, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope and the mountain sheep. Among the animals, you may eat any animal that has the divided hoof [that is, a hoof] split into two parts [especially at its distal extremity] and that chews the cud. 		Ruminants (Ox, Sheep, Goat, Deer) Moderate: Strong herd spatial logic, handler recognition. Lacks abstract reasoning. Low / Natural Biological Safety: Strict herbivorous diets combined with a multi-chambered stomach system and rumination (chewing the cud) provide a safer digestive filtration process. Regurgitating and re-chewing fibrous plant matter mixes it extensively with bicarbonate-rich saliva, maintaining an optimal rumen pH and supporting complex microbial breakdown that filters out multi-host tissue parasites. None (Historical BSE/vCJD risks were isolated anomalies caused entirely by unnatural agricultural feed practices (recycling meat-and-bone meal), not inherent natural zoonosis.)
Deut 14:9
“Of all [creatures] that are in the waters, you may eat these: anything that has fins and scales you may eat,”		 Fin-and-Scale Fish (Trout, Salmon) Low-Mod: Basic navigation, social learning; heavily reliant on instinct. Low: Gills/scales filter pollutants; cold-blooded biology prevents most human-compatible viral hosting. No historical pandemics.
Deut 14:11, 20
“You may eat any clean bird.” 		Clean Birds (Quail etc) Mod-High: High visual memory and complex navigation capabilities. Low: Non-migratory seed-eaters do not scavenge decomposing tissue, minimizing severe pathogen load. No historical pandemics.

2. Unclean Animals (Prohibited to Eat)

Verse (AMP) Animals Intelligence Level & Context Disease Risk Pandemic & Epidemic History
Deut 14:7
“Yet these you shall not eat of those that chew the cud or have the hoof split in two: the camel, the hare, and the coney...”		 Camel High: Exceptional long-term spatial/facial memory; emotional complexity. High Vector: Heavy respiratory shedding acts as a direct bridge to humans. (Note: While camels chew the cud, they possess a modified three-chambered stomach rather than a true ruminant system, and lack a fully split hoof). 2012 MERS-CoV Epidemic
Deut 14:7
(See above verse covering hare/coney)		 Hare / Rabbit Moderate: Cautious, fast-learning prey with sharp spatial tracking. High Vector: Primary vector for Francisella tularensis. Tularemia Outbreaks (Rabbit Fever)
Deut 14:8
“The swine, because it has a divided hoof but does not chew the cud; it is unclean for you...”		 Pig (Swine) Very High: Outperforms dogs in video cognitive tasks; mirror self-awareness. High Vector: Biological 'mixing vessels' with receptors for human/bird flu. Carries Trichinella tissue parasites. 2009 H1N1 Pandemic, 1918 Spanish Flu
Lev 11:27
“Also all animals that walk on their paws, among all kinds of animals that walk on four legs, are unclean to you...”		 Primates / Carnivorans (Monkeys, Apes, Felines, Canines) Extreme to Very High: Self-awareness, social culture, symbolic communication, and complex tool manufacturing/use. High to Extreme Vector: High genetic proximity to humans creates near-zero cross-species barriers for pathogens, allowing viral adaptation and direct transmission via bushmeat handling. HIV/AIDS (SIV crossover), Mpox (Monkeypox), Simian Foamy Virus, Rabies
Deut 14:10
“and you may not eat anything that does not have fins and scales; it is unclean for you.”		 Marine Mammals Extreme to Very High: Advanced tool-use, cultural dialects, and personal signature names. High Vector: Shellfish are filter feeders concentrating toxins, human sewage, and heavy metals. Cetaceans host systemic Brucella ceti. Cholera Epidemics
Deut 14:12-18
“But these are the ones which you shall not eat: the eagle, the vulture, the ospray...” 		Predatory/Carrion Birds (Ravens, Eagles) Very High: Matches great apes in casual, multi-step causal reasoning and tools. High Vector: Feeding on decaying rotting carrion exposes gut to toxic pathogens, shed via feathers/droppings. Avian Influenza (H5N1) Variants
Lev 11:19 / Deut 14:18
“...the stork, all kinds of heron, the hoopoe, and the bat.”		 Bat (Flying things) High / Mod-High: 3D spatial echolocation mapping, long-term roosting memory, and complex social vocalizations. Extreme Vector: World's densest viral mammalian reservoir. Hyper-active immunity allows them to host and shed pathogens symptom-free. Ebola, Marburg, Rabies, SARS-CoV-1, SARS-CoV-2
