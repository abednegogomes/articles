The list below details 100 moral sins ranked by scriptural weight, grounded in the Greek New Testament text and the Amplified Bible Classic Edition (AMPC).

While James 2:10 notes that breaking one law incurs guilt before God, Scripture reveals distinct degrees of gravity—ranging from the unpardonable sin against the Holy Spirit to subtle sins of attitude and speech.

> **Note on "Whitelisted" Laws:** Biblical theology distinguishes between **Moral Law** (unchanging reflection of God's character, e.g., prohibitions on murder, theft, adultery) and **Ceremonial/Civil Law** (purity rites, dietary rules, animal sacrifices, holy days). Dietary restrictions (e.g., eating pork or shellfish) and ceremonial purity rules were fulfilled and set aside in the New Testament (Acts 10:9–15, Mark 7:18–19, Colossians 2:16–17). However, **none of the moral sins listed below are ever whitelisted or permitted in the New Testament.**

### Scriptural Analysis: Sins Fulfillment & "Whitelisting"

In biblical theology, no **moral sin** (such as theft, lying, sexual immorality, murder, or idolatry) is ever "whitelisted" or permitted in the New Testament. In fact, Jesus heightened moral accountability in the Sermon on the Mount by expanding moral standards to internal attitudes (e.g., lust equals adultery; hatred equals murder).

However, Scripture draws a distinction between eternal **moral law** (reflecting God's character) and **ceremonial/civil regulations** (given under the Mosaic Covenant as shadows). Several practices that carried severe penalties in the Old Testament as sins of ceremonial disobedience were fulfilled and reclassified as neutral or clean under the New Covenant:

1. **Dietary Laws (Eating "Unclean" Foods)**
* **Old Testament:** Eating non-kosher foods like pork or shellfish violated ceremonial holiness regulations ([Leviticus 11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus%2011&version=AMPC)).
* **New Testament Status:** Jesus explicitly declared all foods clean, removing ceremonial dietary restrictions.
* [Mark 7:18–19 AMPC](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/%3Fsearch%3DMark%25207%253A18-19%26version%3DAMPC): *"Thus He was making and declaring all foods [ceremonially] clean [that is, abolishing the ceremonial distinctions of the Levitical Law]."*
* [Acts 10:15 AMPC](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/%3Fsearch%3DActs%252010%253A15%26version%3DAMPC): *"What God has cleansed and pronounced clean, do not you call common (unholy or unclean)."*




2. **Strict Seventh-Day Sabbath Observance Regulations**
* **Old Testament:** Doing physical work, traveling beyond designated limits, or gathering firewood on the Sabbath was punishable by death ([Exodus 31:14-15](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/%3Fsearch%3DExodus%252031%253A14-15%26version%3DAMPC)).
* **New Testament Status:** The strict ceremonial Sabbath rules were set aside as symbolic shadows fulfilled in Christ's spiritual rest.
* [Colossians 2:16–17 AMPC](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/%3Fsearch%3DColossians%25202%253A16-17%26version%3DAMPC): *"Therefore let no one sit in judgment on you in matters of food and drink, or with regard to a feast day or a New Moon or a Sabbath. Such [things] are only the shadow of things that are to come..."*
* [Romans 14:5 AMPC](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/%3Fsearch%3DRomans%252014%253A5%26version%3DAMPC): *"One man thinks one day is more important than another, while another considers every day alike. Let every man be fully convinced (satisfied) in his own mind."*




3. **Ceremonial Ritual Washings & Physical Circumcision**
* **Old Testament:** Remaining physically uncircumcised or failing to undergo ceremonial bathings after touching dead objects rendered someone cut off from God's community ([Genesis 17:14](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/%3Fsearch%3DGenesis%252017%253A14%26version%3DAMPC); [Numbers 19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Numbers%2019&version=AMPC)).
* **New Testament Status:** Physical circumcision and ceremonial washings were declared non-essential for covenant standing.
* [Galatians 5:6 AMPC](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians%205%3A6&version=AMPC): *"For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts for anything, but only faith activated and energized and expressed and working through love."*
